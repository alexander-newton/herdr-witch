# The Goetia (The Lesser Key of Solomon, Book I), tr. S. L. MacGregor Mathers, 1904. Source: archive.org transcription.
# The Key of Solomon the King (Clavicula Salomonis), tr. S. L. MacGregor Mathers, 1889. Source: archive.org transcription.
I do invoke thee, and by invocating conjure thee
by Him Who spake and it was done
the air is shaken, the sea runneth back
the fire is quenched, the earth trembleth
Come thou peaceably, visibly, and affably, now, and without delay
manifesting that which I shall desire
by the four beasts before the throne, having eyes before and behind
by the fire round about the throne
and the earth opened, and did swallow up Kora, Dathan, and Abiram
and bind thee in the depths of the Bottomless Pit or Abyss
there to remain unto the Day of the Last Judgment
And I will bind thee in the Eternal Fire
into the Lake of Flame and of Brimstone
and in fire and brimstone unquenchable
I CONJURE thee, O fire, by him who made thee
that thou torment, burn, and consume this Spirit
bury thee in immortal oblivion
in a form comely and fair, and in no wise terrible
Let all the company of Heaven curse thee!
Let the sun, moon, and all the stars curse thee!
into the fire unquenchable, and into the torments unspeakable
and never more to be remembered before the face of GOD
write thou his seal on parchment
shall be choken in sulphurous stinking substances
I conjure thee, O Creature of Fire
by Him who removeth the Earth, and maketh it tremble
that thou burn and torment these Spirits
Be ye accursed, damned, and eternally reproved
and be ye tormented with perpetual pain
so that ye may find no repose by night nor by day
Names which can turn aside lightning and thunder
and which will utterly make you to perish
By these secret Names, therefore, and by these signs
unto a lake of sulphur and of flame
I conjure ye anew, O Spirits, by all the Names of God
by the winds, the whirlwinds, and the tempests
and in all the Abysses of the Shades
we command ye boldly and resolutely
on which all the dry bones in the earth will arise
by that Last Fire which shall consume all things
by the Light and Flame which emanate from His Countenance
among the Mysteries of Mysteries or Secrets of Secrets
we curse ye even unto the Depth of the Great Abyss
we shall cast, hurl, and bind ye
against the Mystery of Mysteries
like a Prince newly crowned entering in triumph
and fifteen cubits above their summits
and will cast ye, ye rebellious Spirits, and unclean beings
bound with eternal chains of fire
Names the armies of the Demons fear, tremble at, and shudder
Come ye, then, without delay, without noise, and without rage
without any deformity or hideousness
for God, strong and powerful, will chase ye and constrain ye
Come ye, come ye, Angels of Darkness
come hither before this Circle without fear, terror, or deformity
which will make ye tremble and quake with fear
ye shall fall into dangerous disease and leprosy
a flame shall go forth from His Mouth
which shall burn ye up and reduce ye unto nothing in Hell
which shall be your ruin and destruction
kindled and taken ineffably from that flame of Divine ardour
Come ye then, come ye, behold the Signs and the Names of your Creator
the Holy Pentacles by the virtue of which the Earth is moved
the trees thereof and the Abysses tremble
I conjure thee anew, O Atmiras, Chief of Invisibility
so that none may be able to see me
Who is seated upon the Throne of His Majesty
banish from my Spirit the darkness of Ignorance
so thick, so awful, and so terrible darkness
Who maketh Earth and Heaven to tremble
Who drewest Moses, Thy well-beloved and Thine elect
the Seal of David, by the Ring and Seal of Solomon
that ye may burn therein eternally for ever
Come thou! for it is ADONAI who commandest thee
the Lake of Fire which is prepared for the damned
who shall come to judge the quick, and the dead
and flames and storm shall surround Him
the Lake of Fire and Brimstone
I do bind thee in the depths of the Bottomless Abyss
and by the Light and Flame which emanate from His Countenance
shall burn ye up and reduce ye unto nothing
ye shall all perish by a terrifying and horrible death
Light of the Angels which is kindled and taken ineffably
